About Our School

Dedicated to Value-Based Education

Teklal Mahto Residential School was established with a clear mission: to provide quality English and Hindi medium education combined with strong moral discipline to students across rural and semi-urban regions.

Our residential setup ensures round-the-clock mentorship where students balance rigorous study hours, personal hygiene, sports drills, and cultural pursuits under dedicated resident teachers.
